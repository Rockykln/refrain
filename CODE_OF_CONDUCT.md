# Code of Conduct

Refrain is a small project with a small contributor base. The expectations
are equally small:

- Be civil. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith from people you don't know yet.
- If a conversation turns hostile, step away rather than escalate.
- No personal attacks, no harassment, no doxxing, no slurs.

Maintainers reserve the right to lock threads, hide comments, and ban
accounts that don't follow the above. There's no tribunal — judgment calls
are made and we move on.

For serious incidents (harassment, threats, etc.) mail
**[contact@rockykln.com](mailto:contact@rockykln.com)**.
